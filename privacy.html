Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information RentoRide collects when you use our site or book a rental, and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and rental dates you submit when booking a vehicle.
  • A masked Aadhaar reference used for identity verification — the full Aadhaar number is never stored on our servers.
  • Payment confirmation details from our payment partner (Razorpay). We do not store your card or UPI details ourselves.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm bookings and coordinate pickup/return over WhatsApp or phone.
  • To process security deposits and refunds.
  • To contact you about your rental, including roadside assistance if needed.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the payment processor needed to complete your transaction, or with authorities if legally required.

4. Data Retention

Booking records are retained for as long as needed for accounting and dispute resolution, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@rentoride.in.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic analytics cookies to understand traffic and improve the service. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@rentoride.in or via WhatsApp.
