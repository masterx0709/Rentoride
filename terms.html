Last updated: August 2026

These terms govern every bike or car rental booked through RentoRide across Rajasthan. By booking a vehicle with us, you agree to the terms below.

1. Eligibility & Documents

Renters must be 18 years or older and hold a valid driving license for the vehicle category being rented. A government-issued ID (Aadhaar) is required for KYC at pickup — only a masked reference is retained, never the full number.

2. Booking & Payment

Bookings are confirmed once availability is verified and the security deposit is collected. Daily rates are shown at the time of booking and apply for a standard 24-hour rental period unless otherwise agreed.

3. Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit is collected before handover. It is returned to your original payment method within 24 hours of the vehicle being returned in its original condition, subject to a damage/fuel-level inspection.

4. Vehicle Use

  • The vehicle must not be used for any illegal activity, racing, or commercial passenger transport.
  • Sub-renting or transferring the vehicle to a third party is not permitted.
  • Helmets (for two-wheelers) must be worn at all times as required by law.

5. Fuel Policy

Vehicles are handed over with a noted fuel level and must be returned at the same level, or a refuelling charge plus a service fee will be deducted from the deposit.

6. Late Returns & Extensions

Extensions must be requested via WhatsApp before the scheduled return time and are subject to availability at the same daily rate. Unapproved late returns are charged per hour beyond the grace period.

7. Damage & Liability

The renter is responsible for any damage, theft, or traffic violation incurred during the rental period. RentoRide's roadside assistance covers mechanical breakdowns but not damage from negligence or misuse.

8. Cancellations

Cancellations made before pickup are eligible for a full deposit refund. Please contact support as early as possible if your plans change.

9. Contact

For questions about these terms, reach us at support@rentoride.in or via WhatsApp.
